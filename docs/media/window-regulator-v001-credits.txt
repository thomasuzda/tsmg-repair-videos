Model qualifications: one generic single-rail cable mechanism is selected. Opposite cable windings share a driven drum; their fixed total lengths are checked against guided carriage travel. The original pane, crossbar and captive carriage move together. A close view exposes the drum edge for teaching. The enclosed reduction mechanism is not claimed as exact gearing. Cable size, damaged outer strand, motor reduction ratio, travel and timing are illustrative; this is not a loaded mechanical simulation or engineering certification. The generic supporting perimeter is an analytical door section, not proven native hidden structure. Only partial travel is shown.

One fault: a damaged outer cable strand catches on the fixed drum-entry guide. The intact cable core, rail and clamps continue to support the glass. Brief motor demand produces a small initial movement, then the drive and glass stop under load; the demand ends rather than a motor rotating indefinitely through a jam. This can leave the window partway open. The specific snag is a teaching example; no universal failure sequence or diagnostic inference is claimed. Electrical supply, control and motor faults can also prevent motion.
