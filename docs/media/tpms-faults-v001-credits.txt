Model qualifications: this film selects a GENERIC battery-powered DIRECT system. Designs, indirect systems, displays and fault behavior vary. The internal geometry and generic receiver are teaching diagrams, not OEM/Camry CAD, exact proprietary circuitry, service instruction or engineering certification. Tire air acts on an illustrative sensing diaphragm; deformation is enlarged. Pressure bars are qualitative and have no PSI, threshold, recommendation or calibration. Battery depletion is compressed for teaching. Radio arcs symbolize wireless data, not a fluid, cable or airline. A battery fault leaves pressure unchanged in the controlled example; unavailable data does not diagnose an exact part or establish tire safety. Low-pressure data is distinguished from one unavailable-signal fault. No warning-lamp flash timing convention is demonstrated.

No repair, replacement, reset/relearn or driving instructions. No universal diagnosis or shop promises. Full source claim map and QA are retained in the project folder.
