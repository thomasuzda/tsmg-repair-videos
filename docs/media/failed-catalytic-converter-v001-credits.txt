Generic gasoline three-way catalyst example, not measured OEM installation CAD. The enlarged analytical section is a separate teaching model. Square channels are open at both ends: this is not a soot-filter demonstration. Molecular icons, sizes, coating thickness, positions and reaction timing are simplified. The balanced carbon-monoxide example conserves atoms: two CO plus O2 become two CO2 at the catalytic surface. Other three-way reactions reduce unburned hydrocarbons and nitrogen oxides, with CO2, water and nitrogen among their products. Conversion depends on temperature, exhaust oxygen/air-fuel balance and catalyst condition; exhaust continues downstream and is not harmless or eliminated. No numeric efficiency is claimed.

One illustrative contamination/loss-of-activity fault is shown with open passages. Oil/coolant contamination can impair the coating. Reduced efficiency need not mean physical blockage or low engine power. Melted/collapsed honeycomb restriction is a separate fault and is not depicted here. Upstream engine faults can damage a catalyst. A warning light, emissions failure, code or symptom alone does not establish a diagnosis.
