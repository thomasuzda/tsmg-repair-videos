Supported explanation: the ignition coil supplies high voltage; the ceramic insulator separates the central conductor from the metal shell; a brief discharge crosses the electrode gap near the end of compression and starts combustion. Rounded, worn electrodes and an increased gap can make ignition unreliable. Rough running, hesitation and a warning light can have other causes.
