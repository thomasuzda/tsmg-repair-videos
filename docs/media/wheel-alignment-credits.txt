Toe and camber examples are exaggerated and labeled. Caster is a steering-axis geometry, not a fore/aft tire lean; no universal caster adjustment is shown. The tire stays fitted to its hub and steering knuckle. Virtual body/tire cutaways expose installed geometry. Setup/service time is condensed; roll compensation and full vehicle-specific equipment procedures are not all animated. Vehicle specifications, load/ride-height setup, available adjustments, required repairs and any calibration follow the individual vehicle. No alignment specification values are invented. No independent specialist has certified this film.
